While hiking in rural Japan, ex-special forces veteran Dax discovers and destroys a Yakuza drug lab. Wounded and being hunted, he makes a desperate run for a train station to evade his pursuers. To hide, he ducks into the women's lavatory on a passenger train, nearly colliding with Ren, a doctor from Tokyo. Seeing his wounds, Ren pulls him inside to hide him from the searching Yakuza. This marks the beginning of their journey.
