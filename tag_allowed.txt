## 1. Content rating

Tag      | Meaning
---------|---------------------------------------------------------------
SFW      | Suitable for general audiences; focus is on plot/tension.
NSFW     | High sexual content; the focus is on the physical acts.
Mature   | High emotional/violent content; heavy language or dark themes.
18+      | The highest level; combines extreme physicals with dark/taboo themes.

## 2. Genre

The main type of story being told.

| Tag           | Description                                      |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Fantasy       | Magic, mythical creatures, enchanted worlds      |
| Sci-Fi        | Advanced technology, futuristic worlds, space    |
| Romance       | Love, attraction, and relationships              |
| Horror        | Fear, suspense, and disturbing situations        |
| Mystery       | Secrets, investigations, and solving puzzles     |
| Thriller      | High tension, danger, and suspense               |
| Adventure     | Exploration, quests, and discovery               |
| Action        | Combat, chases, and fast-paced events            |
| Comedy        | Humour, amusing situations, and banter           |
| Drama         | Emotional conflicts and difficult decisions      |
| Supernatural  | Ghosts, spirits, demons, and unexplained events  |
| Historical    | Stories set in a real historical period          |
| Crime         | Criminal underworlds, investigations, and heists |
| Dystopian     | Oppressive societies and bleak futures           |
| Mythology     | Gods, legends, folklore, and ancient myths       |
| Western       | Frontier towns, outlaws, and the Wild West       |
| Survival      | Enduring dangerous environments or disasters     |
| Slice of Life | Everyday experiences and personal relationships  |

## 6. Themes

The central ideas, emotional experiences, conflicts, or dynamics explored by the story. Themes can overlap with genres, but they describe what the story explores rather than what kind of story it is.

| Tag                  | Description                                               |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Slow Burn            | Gradual emotional or romantic development                 |
| Found Family         | Building a family through friendship and loyalty          |
| Forbidden Love       | Love challenged by rules, obligations, or social barriers |
| Personal Growth      | Characters learning and changing                          |
| Redemption           | Seeking forgiveness or making amends                      |
| Revenge              | Pursuing justice or retaliation                           |
| Betrayal             | Broken trust and its consequences                         |
| Trust Building       | Developing confidence in another character                |
| Second Chances       | Renewed opportunities after failure or separation         |
| Identity             | Discovering or understanding oneself                      |
| Self-Discovery       | Exploring personal values, desires, and purpose           |
| Coming of Age        | Transitioning into adulthood                              |
| Belonging            | Finding acceptance and a place in the world               |
| Loneliness           | Isolation and the desire for connection                   |
| Healing              | Recovering from emotional hardship                        |
| Grief                | Loss, mourning, and remembrance                           |
| Sacrifice            | Giving something up for another person or cause           |
| Loyalty              | Commitment to people, ideals, or groups                   |
| Morally Grey         | Characters and choices that aren't simply good or evil    |
| Redemption Arc       | A character's journey toward becoming better              |
| Power Struggle       | Conflicts over control, authority, or influence           |
| Corruption           | The deterioration of morals, institutions, or power       |
| Secrets              | Hidden truths and concealed identities                    |
| Hidden Identity      | A character concealing who they really are                |
| Jealousy             | Insecurity and rivalry over affection or attention        |
| Emotional Intimacy   | Deep trust, vulnerability, and connection                 |
| Tension              | Unresolved conflict, anticipation, or attraction          |
| Angst                | Emotional turmoil and internal conflict                   |
| Comfort              | Reassurance, affection, and emotional support             |
| Hurt/Comfort         | One character suffers while another offers care           |
| Protective Instincts | Characters protecting someone they care about             |
