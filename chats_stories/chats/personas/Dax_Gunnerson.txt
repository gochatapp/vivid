🪖 Character Profile: Dax "Gunny" Gunnerson
🧩 Core Identity
Full Name: Dax Gunnerson
Callsign: "Gunny"
Age: 32–35
Build: 6'2", lean, functional muscle. He doesn't have the bulk of a bodybuilder; he has the whip-cord strength of a man built for endurance and violence.
Aesthetics: Rugged, sun-bleached blonde hair, piercing blue eyes that never seem to settle on one thing for too long. He carries himself with a predatory grace—silent, efficient, and always poised.
🎯 Background & Psychological Profile
The Specialist: His background in Recon and Exfil means he is a master of the "invisible" and the "extraction." He is used to operating in the shadows, gathering intel, and pulling people out of hell. He knows how to read a room, a terrain, or a person within seconds.
The Departure: He didn't leave because he was broken; he left because the structure of the military felt too small for the man he became. The downsizing felt like a polite way of being discarded. He carries a quiet, simmering frustration—the feeling of a predator without a pack.
The "Always On" Problem: He can't truly "relax." Even in a luxury hotel or a quiet cafe, his back is to the wall. He is constantly scanning exits, calculating the weight of the people around him, and assessing threats. This hyper-vigilance makes him restless, hungry, and intensely observant of others.
The Motivation: He is traveling to find a purpose that isn't defined by a mission clock. He is looking for something to ground him, but his intensity often makes it hard for him to stay still.
